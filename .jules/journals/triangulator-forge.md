 2026-04-24 - Cross-Engine Architectural Deception
Learning: Popularity does not equate to architectural health. vLLM boasts massive community support and hardware compatibility, but its internal architecture is deeply entangled (monolithic event loop mixed with execution logic), making it brittle and difficult to maintain. Conversely, TGI's strict router/server boundary (Rust/Python over gRPC) provides superior operational stability but is often overlooked due to its opinionated ecosystem lock-in.
Action: When designing new inference systems, default to TGI's bounded service architecture (separate routing from execution) rather than vLLM's monolithic async approach, but extract vLLM's superior algorithmic concepts (like PagedAttention) as standalone libraries.
 2026-04-26 - Service-Oriented Integration vs Framework Entanglement
Learning: When comparing high-throughput inference engines, framework entanglement (like vLLM's heavy async loop) offers rapid prototyping but severe operational brittleness. In contrast, strict service-oriented boundaries (like TGI's Rust router via gRPC to Python) provide operational resilience and clear isolation, though at the cost of opinionated lock-in. A high-performance inference project should extract algorithms from monolithic frameworks but adopt architectural boundaries from production-grade service-oriented repositories.
Action: For `sglang-1-bit-turbo`, prioritize extracting vLLM's PagedAttention logic into pure, framework-agnostic utility classes, and isolate the API/batching layer from the execution core using explicit IPC protocols inspired by TGI.
